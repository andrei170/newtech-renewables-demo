The savings figure, in writing, with the assumptions shown

This is the single biggest trust problem in the whole industry, and it is cheap to fix.

Why people distrust solar quotes

Because the savings number is usually said out loud, never written down, and never shows what it assumed about usage, tariff or export rate. A number you cannot check is a number nobody believes.

What we put in writing

The estimate, and underneath it the assumptions it rests on. If your usage or your tariff is different, you can see exactly how the figure moves.

And when it does not stack up

Some roofs and some households do not justify the spend. You will get told that, because a system sold to someone it does not suit generates a complaint rather than a referral.
